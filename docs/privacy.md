# Privacy Policy

**Bubble Level – Spot On** (package `me.saatvik.level`), by Saatvik Sharma.
Effective 7 November 2026.

## The short version

The app collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere. It has no ads, analytics, crash
reporting, accounts or third-party code, and it does not request the Android permission needed to
use the internet.

## What the app uses

- **Motion sensors** (accelerometer, gravity sensor, gyroscope) to show the tilt of your phone.
  Readings are used on the device as they arrive and are never stored or transmitted.
- **Settings you choose** (theme, units, tolerance, mode, vibration, keep-screen-on) are saved in the
  app's private storage on your phone. If you use Android's own backup, Android may include these
  settings in your device backup; that backup is managed by your phone and your Google account, and
  the developer has no access to it.
- **Calibration** is saved in private storage that Android excludes from backups and device
  transfers, because it describes the sensor of one particular phone.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of the above.

## Sharing

No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose tool and collects no data from anyone, including children.
